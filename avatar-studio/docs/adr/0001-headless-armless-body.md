# The Body is headless and armless, ending in Caps at the Cuts

The first brief asked for a full figure; the owner then asked for the model without head and arms. The Body therefore ends at a Cut through the neck and through each shoulder, and a Cap closes every Cut.

This is hard to reverse because Garments, Motions, Dynamics and every Trait group assume that no head or arms exist: there are no face Traits, hair, ears, cuffs or sleeves that reach past the shoulder.

## Consequences

- Outfits drop headgear and cuffs; the Bunny Suit keeps its collar, bow, tights, heels and tail, the Maid Dress keeps its puffed shoulders, apron and stockings.
- Accessories attach only to the neck, torso, hips, legs and feet.
- The Body must still read as an adult woman without a face, so Age never goes below 25 and the Base Layer is mandatory.
