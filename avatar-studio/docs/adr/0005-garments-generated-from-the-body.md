# Garments are generated from the Body, not imported

Shells are cut out of the Body's own surface and pushed outward by the fabric thickness; Drapes are built from the Body's Measurements and then simulated. Imported meshes are used only where they fit by nature (shoes from MakeHuman's CC0 pack).

No CC0 Bunny Suit or Maid Dress exists, and an authored garment cannot fit every Trait combination, let alone the Extended Range. Generating the Garment from the Body guarantees the fit by construction. The cost is less hand-sculpted detail and more code, and a new Outfit is a recipe, not a model file.
